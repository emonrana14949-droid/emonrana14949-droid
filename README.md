## About Me

I am a final-year BSc in CSE student at Southeast University, Bangladesh. I am a co-author of a final year design project studying how generative AI affects students' creativity and academic performance, based on a questionnaire survey of university students. I also build embedded and IoT systems — from GPS-based rescue tracking to smart water tank automation — and practice data analysis with Python and NumPy.

- **University:** Southeast University, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
- **Research:** Generative AI in education, survey design and data analysis
- **Hands-on:** ESP32, Arduino, sensors, IoT dashboards, Python notebooks
- **Currently:** Preparing the main survey for our FYDP and prototyping CSE 382 embedded systems projects
