## 👋 About Me

<img align="right" width="300" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPCQ/giphy.gif" alt="Coding GIF" />

Hi, I'm **Md Emon Rana** — a **BSc in Computer Science and Engineering student at Southeast University, Dhaka, Bangladesh**.

I'm building my career in **Data Science, AI, and Machine Learning**. I work with **Python, NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib** to analyse data, visualise patterns, and learn how models think.

> 🔬 **Research:** Co-author on a final-year design project studying how **generative AI affects students' creativity and academic performance** — a questionnaire survey of university students *(pilot: 100 respondents)*.

I also build **embedded & IoT prototypes with ESP32 and Arduino** — smart water tanks and GPS rescue trackers.

<br clear="right"/>

- 🎓 **Education:** BSc in CSE — Southeast University, Dhaka, Bangladesh
- 📊 **Focus:** Data Science, AI & Machine Learning
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Machine Learning with Python — Pandas, scikit-learn and beyond
- 🔌 **Also into:** Embedded systems & IoT — ESP32, Arduino
